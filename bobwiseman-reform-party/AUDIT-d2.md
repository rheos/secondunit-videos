## Themes (what several critics found independently)
1. **The chairs still don't read as hop-on height** (A, film). At 720p the suits read as standing men on stilts; the car
   under the table doesn't read. Short brackets, knees up, feet near the sand, wheels and a dust trail.
2. **Same framing half the film** (film: ~70 of 135 samples are the side-on table; ~6 distinct framings). Intro (0:00-0:16)
   and instrumental (1:00-1:14) are near-identical static wides (A, film). Every spin refrain is the same high wide.
3. **Refrains rerun instead of escalating** (A, B, C, film). The beige blind holds 3-6 s and reads as a dead frame or a
   loading bar, three times the same. The spin is far and small each time. Grin is the same close-up.
4. **The climax is not the biggest image** (D, film, guest). The Man burns small in a far wide for ~20 s; no figure
   inside the fire; the four outro refrains don't escalate; ballots too small to read.
5. **Dusk 3:16-3:39 is mud-dark and static** (D); the spiral procession doesn't read.
6. **Light arc flat** (film): blue noon from 0:16 to 3:12, then dusk in one go. No golden hour.
7. **Burning Man density still reads as clip-art backdrop** (all): the same domes, towers, clock-cart parked in every
   wide; elephants invisible at 720p; the bike riders are crude blocks (worst-drawn objects in the film); nothing out
   on the playa reacts to the words.
8. **Hard cuts, not match cuts** (A, B, D, film). Good exceptions: chart bars to cell bars, the hat hole.
9. **Key words land small or late**: gavel on "law" a few pixels; "unveiled/jails" pulls out to a sliver; teeth late
   and small after "soundbite"; G20 cops read as bollards and are never paid; poor person reads as a cyclist; the dime
   is a grey disc; "perverse" shows nothing; the giant gavel lands beside the table, tiny.

## Guest director top picks (ranked for Bob)

   and catches on each "vote" (3:40.6, 3:50.1, 3:59.6, 4:09.3). [Nakamura route + Gilliam crowd]
2. Dusk spiral that tells a story: ~60 walkers on a visible spiral with lanterns, inhabited props along it (ballot box
   on stilts, newspaper kite, recorder on crutches); the spiral tightens into the Man's base. [Nakamura N20]
3. The Gilliam chain in verse 2: chart bars -> cell bars on "crime" -> rows of chattering teeth on "soundbite" -> the jail
   model on "unveiled" -> cells on stilts on "jails", each morph in the same screen spot. [Gilliam G05]
4. The padlocked ear: the tape lines run into the ear canal, a drawer slides shut in it, a padlock drops on "restrict".
   [Von Ulrich U06]
5. A world inside the table: push through the driver's hole into a miniature playa (turnstile, the bowl on "poor"),
   then pull out. [Nakamura N27]
6. Escalate each "grin": refrain 1 one egg-lid lifts; refrain 2 the row lifts on the beat; refrain 3 lids lift, wind-up
   teeth climb out, then the gavel drops. [Gilliam move 4]
