## Themes (strongest signal = several agents independently)

1. **Untie the drone** (film, all four critics, scout). One new camera mode: world-anchored key windows that blend in
   and out of the car-relative flight over about 1.5 s (the shape `camera-end.js` already uses). Everything in themes
   2-4 needs it. The scout measured the geometry: the 9:00 promenade sits 39-51 m behind the car all day (a free
   stage), the Esplanade is 450-620 m away and reachable in about 14 s each way at 70-90 m up without looking fast,
   Center Camp is about 1 km away (aerial only).
2. **Excursions where the city acts the line** (the roam windows the agents agreed on):
   - Intro: open over the Esplanade at sunrise (a sound camp still dancing, bikes streaming out), glide out and land
     on the gavel (film, scout; critic A prefers opening on the Man).
   - Instrumental 1: an impromptu jam the sax rider pedals into (film), or the Thunderdome (A).
   - "build more jails": a build crew 25 m off the car (B).
   - Refrain 2 out along the 9:00 promenade (scout): shade cloth pulled across the city's sky on "blues", a real
     whiteout on "restrict / news" (tan, never white), suits in the Thunderdome grinning on "grin", the spin seen from
     80 m up on "spin", home for "vogue".
   - Instrumental 2: a blues jam under a shade that a suit blinds (B), or the bunny march (scout), or Dali's ant
     burners carrying a giant ballot (Dali).
   - Bridge: the ice line at Center Camp on "poor / share / unfair" (C); the Thunderdome on "wars / bets" (C).
   - Refrain 3 out over the city at golden hour (film): the red curtain drops across the whole horizon, papers blow
     toward the Esplanade, 40 bike lights spiral round the car on "spin", the gavel, straight up into the aerial.
   - Descent: through the Fire Conclave and onto the drummers as they come in (D, film).
   The Thunderdome came up three times; it should appear once. Recommended home: "wars / bets" (two suits fight, the
   board bets), or "grin 2" if refrain 2 goes out.
3. **The outro speaks for the wrong people** (film, D). "We" is the people, but the suits hold up the ballots.
   Fix: the crowd votes (one more row each repeat); the suits collect the ballots and fold them into party hats on
   "joke" (D: the "party" pun, deadpan). The poor woman from the bridge sits in the front row and her bowl fills with
   ballot ash on "broke".
4. **The burn is a town bonfire, and the board breaks the rules** (scout, D, film):
   - No vehicle moves inside a burn perimeter and there are no Rangers in the film. Make it the joke: Rangers kneel on
     the line facing the crowd, the board is the only thing inside, flashlights on it as it leaves unlit (set up by a
     DAY licence plate dropping on "law"). Or park it with the art cars behind the crowd, suits standing on their
     chairs.
   - The crowd is 860 people 15 m deep; the real one is ten-plus rows (2017 coverage: about 50,000). Thicken it as
     instanced dots for the aerial.
   - After the fall the crowd walks away into the dark. The real moment is the opposite: the perimeter drops and the
     crowd rushes in and circles the fire counter-clockwise.
   - The lamplighters run backwards: lamps light outward from the Man at 52 m/s like streetlights. Real: they leave
     Center Camp at a walk and light each spire by hand; processional spires carry four lanterns (the single-lantern
     post reads as a gibbet at 3:18).
   - The drum circle belongs with the Fire Conclave, not beside the board; it is never framed anyway.
   - No dust weather anywhere.
5. **Refrains escalate on the table but the world never grows** (film, C). Refrain 3 is the most enclosed (from inside
   the hat hole) exactly when it should be the biggest. Theme 2's refrain 3 excursion fixes it.
6. **The bridge glide is a regression** (C). The gaussian glide averages three or four bridge framings that are 1.1-1.6
   s apart, so the boom mics, the chips and the truth never get their close-ups. Add exact windows for the bridge.
7. **Dali objects are parked, not acting** (film). About 12 Dali objects are set dressing; four act on a word. Give

   swallows a paper on "news".

## The directors' symbolic thinking (Robin's second ask)

Three of the four guests arrived independently at the same spine: **the board confiscates what it silences, and the
vote releases it.** That is the strongest idea in the audit. Each built it differently:

- **Von Ulrich, "confiscate, then wear."** Five burners in big costume heads walk beside the board all film (a real
  playa custom): a reel-to-reel head, a radio head, a press-camera head, a mic head, a magnifying-glass head. All five
  die on "can't". The board takes one head per lyric and in refrain 3 wears them as crowns on its blank eggs. "The
  board has no face at all without stolen equipment." On the third "vote" the heads hop off back to their owners, and
  on the fourth the five frame the burning Man with raised ballots. Plus: same face, new hat (every "spin" changes one
  thing on all 14 eggs at once: pinwheels, weathervanes, then the stolen crowns).
- **Nakamura, "the ark in the drawers."** Everything silenced goes into the drawer-torso suit: the scientists, the
  bird, the truth. At the burn a trickster, a burner in a jackrabbit hood the drone keeps finding around the city,
  picks the drawers and everything spills out to the crowd. His deeper point: Burning Man has no money, so the
  board's dime, chips and paid coins are worthless here and "people that are broke" describes everyone in the city.
  Plus a bluebird that is caught in the blind, caged, freed at the burn and lands on Little Bob's piano, and a
  thaumatrope (bird on one side, cage on the other, spinning puts the bird in the cage: spin made literal), bigger
  each refrain.
- **Gilliam, "the system as plumbing."** A silver dryer duct runs from under the table and sucks the music out of the
  city on each "blues" (a harmonica, then a sound camp whose dancers freeze, then the sax rider left blowing silence);
  it bursts on vote 1 and the notes fly into the night. The Citizen, a little man with cardboard wings and a lab coat,
  recurs: his recorder is taken, his flight fails on the sax lick, he is boxed and stamped on "truth in a box", climbs
  out at vote 3 and flies off the embers chasing a paper bird folded from the board's minutes. On "rehearsed" all 14
  egg lids pop to show the confiscated recorders turning in sync inside the heads.
- **Dali, "spin is the paranoiac-critical method used against the public."** One picture painted to read two ways,
  with power choosing where you stand; the drone flies to the other spot and shows the second picture. Three double
  images, each found by a camera move: the board's face on "grin 3" (from above the table is a mouth, the eggs are
  teeth, the red hat the tongue), the shadow jail on "jails" (the cells' long shadows fall across the board like
  bars), and the people made into a joke on outro 3 (the front rows' raised ballots read as a grin from 26 m; on
  "people" every fourth falls out like a knocked-out tooth). Plus: the mahogany table is the film's one hard thing and
  it finally goes soft in the outro; ants walk out of the bite in the table on "soundbite".
- **The scout, "the party behaves like a first-timer who thinks the rules are for other people."** Every real ritual
  is the opposite of the party: gifting against the coin, the perimeter against the VIP seat, the lamplighters
  against the blind over the sky, unpaid Rangers against the paid pawns. The contrast is the joke, and it only works
  if the city is exactly right.

These compete for the same screen time, so the film wants one spine plus moments from the others. Double images and
the scout's rituals are moments and fit any spine.
