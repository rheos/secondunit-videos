Buh sync: the teeth live over the picture

Pick a run and press Play. The teeth are drawn live over the picture, so every change shows straight away. The strip under the picture is the beat grid: one cell per 16th note, a thin line on each beat, a thick line on each bar. Gold cells are buhs. Click a cell to add or remove a buh; hold the mouse and drag to paint several in, or out. Click the time ruler on top of the strip to jump there. Slow it down to check an edge. When it looks right, tell Claude "done".
