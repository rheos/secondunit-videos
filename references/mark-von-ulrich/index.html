Patterns to investigate

Perception becomes anatomy: ear → listening; hand with eyes → embodied judgment; screen-eye → mediated vision.

Abstract systems become bodies: the VR player is assembled from interface elements, and the conversation becomes a shared signal field.

One body can carry a series of ideas: the simplicity chart repeats the same face and changes the head structure to compare approaches.

Radiance is flexible: bursts recur around different subjects. Treat them first as energy or emphasis; spiritual meaning is unverified.

These are comparative observations, not claims about the artist’s private intentions.
