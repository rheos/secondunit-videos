# Bob Wiseman, "All The Trees"

On *In Her Dream: Bob Wiseman Sings Wrench Tuttle* (2024 reissue; YouTube hNdJCOJNtto), 2:33. A surprise for Bob.

Look: wood marquetry, drawn in code. Inlaid veneers with grain anchored to each piece, flush glue seams, a lacquer sheen.
Thinking after Mark Von Ulrich (frontal emblems, one constant face with a different crown, relationships made physical), not his surface.
Heads are end-grain (oyster) slices with knot eyes.
