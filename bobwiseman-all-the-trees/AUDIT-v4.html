  1. Concealed instrument; listening and a shared glance.
  2. Preparation inside the cloak/cloud; horn stays hidden.

PRIORITY 1 · Music

Let the first horn be a surprise

A trumpet emblem appears at 8 seconds; the bird introduced at 10.458 seconds also carries one. Heralds arrive in an already-playing pose. The current rig has no conceal, prepare, play, breath or rest states.

Direct the revision

Show the heralds listening or conspiring with hands and horns concealed by a cloak or cloud. At the verified first brass attack, reveal the horn and its first ray together. A nearby listener recoils, catches their balance, and passes the reaction onward. In phrase gaps, lower the instrument and recover.

Sound wakes a connected world. Concealment gives the revealed faculty a consequence.

PRIORITY 1 · Cast

Give the ensemble different jobs

There are eight people by the last chorus, but the six additions reuse the same two rigs with different colours, scale and phase. More arrivals do not add new motives or useful abilities.

Direct the revision

Add four functional roles: a listener with an enlarged receptive feature; a carrier/grafter whose hands bridge a gap; a caretaker whose branch-body shelters visitors; a small mischief-maker who tugs the network. Each must change what another character can do.

A community works because its members contribute differently.

PRIORITY 1 · Acting

Make contact interrupt the dance

The people repeat roughly a 0.828-second sine cycle. Arms, heads and bodies move, but they rarely intend, resist, catch or recover. A bird lands on the moving hand geometry without its weight changing the host’s action.

Direct the revision

One person offers a branch; the other tests its spring and almost overbalances. Their roots catch the fall. A bird landing bends the bough, interrupts the dance and makes its host adjust. Let the next musical answer release the stored spring.

Being a tree is a usable bodily condition with obligations, not just an identity badge.

The environment changes what connection requires of the characters.

PRIORITY 2 · Story

Make the heart perform care

The heart grows from the roots, which is a useful physical origin, then becomes a persistent central sign. The instrumental adds orbiting curves without changing the relationship between the cast.

Direct the revision

Treat the heart as a living pump or bellows. One figure supplies warmth, another shelters a neighbour; a phrase pushes warmth through one root and a bare branch opens at its destination. During the instrumental, the network briefly becomes a bodily orchestra, then returns to the grove.

Love is something the community does that lets another body recover or grow.

Evidence, Mark reference and review check

Protect

  • Principal sun/moon faces, modest robes and readable joints.
  • Substantial roots, visible joins and planted weight.
  • Untouched pinned soundtrack and deterministic song-time rendering.

Retire these defaults

  • An always-playing mouth-to-horn silhouette.
  • One dense global onset envelope treated as a trumpet score.

  • Looped bobbing used as the principal dramatic action.
  • Weather badges that do not affect anyone.
  • A root connection erased for an unrelated entrance.
