## What the registry's setters could not do (done with raw properties of `obj` or `it`)

- **Every volume that falls toward the lens** (`redwood`, `bottle`, `glass_far`, `pepper`, `basket`, `watchtower_b`):
  `fold(k, heading)` always lands painted side up, so a card falling toward the lens spins half a turn in the first
  third of its fall. `foldVolume` in the skin lays those face down instead (the card is two-sided). It is used for
  all eight volumes so they move alike. A `fold(k, heading, faceDown)` would cover it.
- **`brick_NN`** has no hinge: `rotation.order = "YXZ"`, then `rotation.x`.
- **Standees** (`treeman`, the citizens' fallback): `fold(k, dir)` sets the hinge from upright, so at k = 0 it would
  stand a card that already lies (the guards and bricklayers the wall took down) back up. I blend from the pose the
  owner gave it this frame.
- **`sapling`**: it lies folded by its owner, but its card still turns to the lens and leans back under a high lens,
  so from above it would lift 32 degrees off the cloth. Its `face` rotation is set flat.
- **Fence cards lying on a plate**: the disc is drawn with a polygon offset, which at 190 cm wins over a card 0.15 mm
  above it. The cards are raised 0.9 mm as they land.
- **The plate discs** have no colour setter: the pale disc is `material.color`, set after the table has set it.
- **`wall_NN`**: section 7 felled the wall outward, so it overhangs the table's edge by 3.7 cm all round. No setter
  brings it in; it fades where it lies. From above it is a brick border outside the cloth from f4500 to f4629.
- **The room** (`room_wall`, `room_floor`) has no dim and its colour is not set every frame by its owner, so I cannot
  change it without breaking the rule that a frame is a function of f. The room goes dark through `grade.expo`.

## Where this departs from the brief, and why

- **The redwood lies across the fence line.** It stands outside the chamfer with 7 cm of cloth behind it and is 45 cm
  tall: there is nowhere outside the fence it can lie on the cloth. It comes down along the right side of the city
  (toward [30, -14], clear of the bottle and the candle) and the cutlery cards under its trunk are pushed flat inward
  as the trunk reaches their tops (`pushed()`), 79 frames before the rest of the fence hinges down.
- **The fence hinges down inward**, onto the cloth, not outward over the table's edge as it was dealt: outward the
  cards would hang in the air beyond the cloth and could not "fade to blank cloth".
- **The camera looks left on "remind" and right on "species".** The join at f4315 looks 28 degrees right. The couple
  are section 8's and its builder had not placed them when I built: I aimed at the left edge at z about 14, which is
  where the f3840 join puts them. If they stand elsewhere on the left edge, move the target of the f4375 and f4389 keys.
- **Camera speed** is about 0.9 to 1.2 cm a frame from f4360 to f4560 (the rise is 196 cm in 274 frames), then 0.12.
- **The hooks pin the light to f4314.** `flame`, `fill` and `grade` read the film's value at f4314 and at f (from
  `story.js`, loaded lazily, with my own hooks switched off) and return the factor that holds f4314's state. So the
  section starts from section 9's guttered candle and grey-blue grade whether or not its hooks hold past f4315 (they
  do today). The end state (`PALE`) is absolute.
- **Grey-blue to the end.** I did not bring any colour back for the bare table: it is section 9's grey-blue by a
  third of a candle, then out. One line in `grade` would warm it if wanted.
