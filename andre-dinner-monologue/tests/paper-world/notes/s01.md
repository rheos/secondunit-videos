## What the section does to the table's pieces (inside f0 to f196 only)

- `flame` hook: `hinge` 1 until f12, then the spring; `lit` 0 until f12, then the opening with its flicker, and the sink
  on "bored"; `h` (small as it catches, half on the sink). No change from f196 on.
- `fill` hook: 0.5 in the dark, up to 1 as the light opens (f12 to f23). 1 from there on.
- `candle` part `flame`: the card swings a few degrees past upright (the hook clamps at upright, so the skin sets the
  rotation for those frames).
- `key_light`, `bounce_light`: their `distance` (the reach of the light) grows from the flame outward for 24 frames
  from f12, then is 0 (no limit) again. The skin sets it on every frame of the film, because the registry does not put
  it back: a later section that wants its own reach sets it after section 1's update.
- Every painted volume and flat of section 5 (plates, glasses, bottle, shakers, basket, sapling, cutlery): its tint is
  scaled by the same reach for f12 to f35, so a piece lights when the light gets to it, not before.
- `fg_glass`, `fg_fork` (section 5's, standing at [7, -10] and [9, -38]): hidden for f0 to f195. From this lens the
  glass stands in front of the candle.

## Where this departs from the brief, and why

- **The lens is never over the near plate.** The joins put it 31 cm outside the cloth's near edge at f0 and 5 cm outside
  at f196, moving in at both. One push in between them cannot go over the plate and come back. The key image is from low
  behind the near plate.
- **The soft tableware stands off the table.** For the same reason there is no table under the lens for it to stand on.
  The two copies stand in the air between the lens and the cloth's edge, with their feet (and the glass plate's cut top)
  outside the frame for as long as they are drawn. They read as soft foreground, not as objects with a place.
- **No hinge line is drawn at the wick.** In the dark it would not be seen (pieces are at 6 %), and lit it is inside the
  glow. From this distance the flame card is about 30 px tall: the flip reads as the flame catching, not as a card
  turning. A check from 25 cm shows the hinge: `out/paper-world/wip/s01_hinge.jpg` (f8, 12, 13, 14, 16, 18, 20, 24).

## Needed outside these files

- **The join at f196.** `fg_glass` and `fg_fork` come back at [7, -10] and [9, -38] on f196 unless section 2 hides them

- **The room wall** (`room_wall`, section 5's): fine vertical pinstripes show in its dark areas, clearest in the dark
  opening (f0 to f11) and in every wide frame.
