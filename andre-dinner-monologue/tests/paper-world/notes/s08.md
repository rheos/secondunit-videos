## What it is

- **The couple**: one standee from the `couple` plate (two figures and two suitcases on one base), 4.5 cm high and
  5.28 cm long. It is painted facing right and travels left, so from the near side the lens sees its mirrored
  back, facing the way it goes. It stays one card for the whole film.
- **The slot**: one straight cut, 30 cm, from [-14.5, 47.6] beside the near plate tower to [-43.5, 39.8], 1.5 cm
  short of the cloth's left edge, where a short bar is cut across it (the stop). It is cut as they go, 1.7 cm
  ahead of the tab. It crosses the fence line at z 40.6 and the fallen wall segment beyond it; over the wall
  the cut is in the wall card.
- **Why the near left**: the basket's and the shakers' shadows cover the left edge from z 17 to z 46, and the
  near plate tower's covers the near edge right of x -18. The strip between them is the one way to the left
  edge where the couple's own shadow reaches the cloth. The slot turns 15 degrees toward the far side, so a
  lens level with them on the near side has the basket, the shakers, the glass tower, the candle and the bottle
  behind them.
- **The gate**: on "out" the two fence cards either side of the slot (`fence_41` the knife, `fence_42` the
  spoon) hinge down outward onto the fallen wall, three frames apart, damping 0.62. They stand again from f3815
  to f3833, behind the lens.
- **The far tables**: ten soft cut-outs of the `far_tables` plate, feathered to nothing at an ellipse, blurred
  2 to 5 px and dimmed to 50 to 74 %, each with a small additive glow on its flame. The whole plate stands 233
  cm out as four tables on one plane (200 cm wide); six more are cut from it and stand 124 to 236 cm out at
  other bearings, the nearer ones lower in the frame. A table is not drawn until its candle catches (a spring, damping 0.5, a little over, then a
  3 % flicker). The engine's `room` card already runs all the way round the table, so it is the wall behind them.

## Where this departs from the brief, and why

- **The gate is not in the brief.** With the fence standing, a card 5.3 cm long in a 4.7 cm gap is always
  half behind a fence card from the side, and from behind it is seen through two of them. Two cards hinge down.
- **The shadow bias.** The engine's bias (0.012 of a 600 cm range) starts every shadow 7 cm behind its card.
  72 cm from the flame, with the lens 13 cm from the couple, their shadow began under the lens and the cloth
  between was bare. In this section the bias eases to -0.004 (2.4 cm). No acne at 1:1 (`key-image_1to1_f3613.png`).
- **"Behind them" is 48 degrees off side-on, on their near side**, not straight behind: straight behind, a card
  that travels at right angles to the edge is a line.
- **At the stop the card stands across the fence line**, the woman outside it and the man in the gate: there
  are 4.5 cm of cloth outside the fence and the slot ends 1.5 cm short of the edge.
- The descent crosses the near fence 9 cm above its cards and comes down inside it. Passing between two of them
  at 3 cm filled the frame with a knife.
