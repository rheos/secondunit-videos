## How it is built

- The lid is section 5's `plate_near`, lifted every frame with its own `lid` setter and then swung round the plate's
  centre to the hinge (raw position and quaternion of `obj`, turned back in its own plane so the paint does not move).
- `s02.hinge` (the cut, drawn with `X.slot`), `s02.lid_tab` (a paper tab under the lid's rim), `s02.tub` (the floor of
  the opening, its lip and slits, the front wall with the waves and the duck on it, the back wall), `s02.child` (a
  standee made with `X.standee`, on a tab; nothing is added to the figure).
- The tub is the `bathtub` plate cut in two along the water's near edge: the front card is the wall and the near rim,
  the back card is the tub without its feet. The waves strip stands 1.4 cm behind the front wall.
- The floor of the opening is lit and takes shadows like the cloth (it is ground, not a painted piece).
- Hooks: `grade` warms the picture 3.5 % and opens it 5 % from f244 to f502, eased to nothing at both ends.

## Where this departs from the brief, and why

- **The lid is hinged on the far-left of the rim, 55 degrees round from the straight-far point.** Hinged straight
  across the far edge, the open lid (24 cm wide, 23 cm tall) stands between the tub and the candle for every low
  camera in front of the plate, and the near glass at [12, 30] closes the only gap beside it: the key image ("the
  candle beyond") cannot be had. Turned 55 degrees, the lid stands behind the child and the flame shows past its edge.
- **The camera circles right first, then back to the left** (-11 degrees at the join, 19 at f290, -9.5 at f461, -27 at
  the join at f504), and rides 8 to 9.5 cm high from "bathtub": lower than that the flame is behind the tub's far rim.
- **The back wall lifts on a slit; only the front wall hinges.** Two walls lying flat both ways do not fit in an 11 cm
  plate round a child who is already standing.
- **The duck comes up from behind the front wall**, not in from the side: the tub's end is too narrow to hide it.
- The duck is seen from its back (the plate mirrored), so it faces the child.
