## What it is

One pull-strip of cream card carries the whole machine: a spine along the far left (x = -32.6), three hinge
strips (the ranks, z = -50, -54.5, -59), an arm to the wheel's hub at [-27, -31] and a tab to the television at
[-35.5, -42.5]. It slides in flat over the far edge (f508 to f558), pops up, runs, falls asleep, and is drawn
off the same way (f1198 to f1242). Nothing of it is drawn beyond the far edge of the cloth.

- 24 citizens in three ranks of eight, 6 riders on the wheel: one plate (`citizen`), one grey-ochre, 4 cm.
- The coin is a real disc (the plate's face on a circle, a gold edge) on a paper hub; it lifts 1.7 cm and turns
  four whole turns, so every rider ends where it started.
- The television is a volume (it turns to the lens); its dark-screen twin is made from the plate. The screen is
  its own light: a cold point light on the cloth and a cold tint on the painted pieces by distance from it.

## Where this departs from the brief, and why

- **"Slots show in the cloth" is the pull-strip sliding in.** Cuts in the cloth could only leave by a fade, and
  nothing of this section may be left on the table at f1800 (sections 5 to 7 are compared byte for byte). So the
  hinge lines are strips of one card that arrives and leaves by the brief's own pull-strip.
- **The ranks are at x -30.8 to -16.5, not -38 to -15.** The join at f1196 looks at [-30, 2.2, -50], which has
  to be the left end of the front rank; the far fork lies at x -15 to -12. Spacing 2.05 cm, z -50, -54.5, -59.
- **The wheel stands in front of the ranks** (toward the viewer), the screen between its far left rim and the
  front rank. In the 9:16 frame they stack: wheel, screen and ranks, room above.
- The slide-in and the pull-off ease over 50 and 44 frames, not 10 to 14: they travel 57 and 60 cm.
- Section 5's `fg_glass` and `fg_fork` are hidden for f504 to f1195 (coordinator's instruction).

## BANNED additions (found here)

- A plate's side sliver mapped onto a turning disc: it reads as a dark blob going round. Map the face only.
- `recolour` leaves the plate's pale cut rim in the old colour: repaint it when every card must be one colour.
- A flat card resting at the cloth's height: its back face fights the cloth. Rest it a millimetre up.
- A light left in the scene at zero outside its frames: take it out of the scene (`visible = false`).

## Defects and limits

- f556 to f566: the camera arrives over the flat coin; it fills the lower right of the frame until f568.
- f1151 to f1195: the join's camera stands 17 cm off, so the last citizen is about 250 px tall in the upper
  half of the frame, with bare cloth below it.
- The riders are flat card tangent to the rim: each goes edge-on to the lens twice a turn.
- The cold tint on the ranks is slight; the cold light on the cloth near the screen carries most of it.
- f1196 to f1242 are seen by section 4's camera: the coin crosses the lower frame of its stub camera at f1200 to f1215.
