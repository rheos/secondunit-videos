  Its outline is cut on "meeting"; it flips over about its back edge on "just"; the back wall springs up off the floor
  on "met"; the side walls swing open off the back wall 9 and 12 frames later.
- **The television (6 cm), the newspapers and the magazines** are cards flush against the back wall. On its word a
  dashed cut runs round each (8 frames, drawn from the foot round the outline traced from its own alpha), then it tips
  back about its foot through the wall (12 frames, one small bounce) and lies behind it. The wall is then the same
  painting with a hole in that outline and a pale cut edge.
- **The flap**: the two painted doors are cut out of the wall and hinge into the room on their outer edges; the painted
  latch is its own card and flips up first. Behind them the `eye` plate alone, 10 cm wide, in three layers: the white
  (the iris painted out), the iris on its slide, the lid. A lamp card behind it and a cool pool on the room's floor.
- **The physicist**, 5 cm, on a slot from just outside the open front to the middle of the room; his soft shadow on the
  room's floor is a card (a painted floor takes no real shadow).
- **The citizen**, 4 cm, section 5's red plate, with the `robot` plate on the back of the same card, on its own slot.

## Where this departs from the brief, and why

- **The room opens 25 degrees right of straight-near, and the camera works from its near side**, inside the pocket
  between the room, the pepper pot, the salt and the basket. The salt and the pepper stand where a camera on the room's
  near right would be, and section 5's first key needs the camera to arrive travelling away from the viewer, so the way
  out is round the near side of the pepper pot: it and the salt pass close to the lens from f1780 to f1791.
- **The citizen stops at [-17.5, -7.5]**, not [-14, -10], so the lens can see it past the pepper pot.
- **The room rises between "just" and "met" (f1252 to f1300)**, not from f1196: the camera has to come round from the
  far left corner first. "meeting" is the cut of the floor's outline.
- **The robot does not turn back to the citizen**: it slides away as the robot, the way it came (out of frame).
- **The flap shuts and the room folds after the camera has left** (it cannot see both the robot and the flap).
- **Slots close** (the cut heals from its far end) rather than leave by a mechanism: f1826 to f1838, out of frame.

## BANNED (added for this section)

- A face round the eye, lashes, a brow: the eye is the `eye` plate alone behind the flap.
- A person built from code-drawn parts. A readable word, headline or logo on the newspapers, the magazines or the screen.
- Relighting a painted piece: the lamp's light is an additive pool on the floor and a lit card, never a light on the paint.
- A cut-out that vanishes: each tips back through its own hole and lies behind the wall until it folds back.
- The camera through a wall, a door or a standee; a zoom.
