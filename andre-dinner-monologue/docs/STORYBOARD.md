# Storyboard: the pop-up table (whole film, 4761 frames, 198.4 s, 9:16)

One world: a restaurant table at night, cream cloth, one candle, set for two in paper. Nobody sits at
it. Everything Andre says pops up out of the place setting and folds away again. Method and look rules:
`LOOKS.md` sections 4 to 6. Frames are 24 fps film frames from `_in/cues.json`; exact hits are snapped
to onsets at build time. Words are named by key word only; the transcript stays out of git.

## The grammar (every section uses only these)

| Move | What it is | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Hinge | a card lying flat on the cloth swings up to standing (and back down) | arrivals and exits of scenery |
| Slot | a standee on a tab slides along a slot cut in the cloth, wobbling a little on twos | every person; nobody walks with legs |
| Turn | a card turns about its vertical axis; edge-on for two frames; the back is a different picture | citizen to guard, citizen to robot, glass to tower |
| Pull-tab | a strip slides and something linked to it moves | the split head, the bricks over the eyes |
| Wheel | a disc turns under a window or carries standees round | the boredom machine |
| String | a small piece hangs or drops on a thread from above | the seed, the stars |
| Fold | a honeycomb or accordion piece opens out and closes | the pine, the last burst |

Camera: one continuous move per section, low and close, drifting when it slows. Sections join through a
paper event (a fold closing, the candle guttering, a card passing the lens), never a plain fade. Light:
the candle is the key. It dims and steadies with the speech; it goes out on the last word.

## Pieces (painted cut-outs unless noted)

(plate rim, fork, glass stem), candle and flame, plate disc, tiered plate tower, glass standee and its
tower back, bottle standee and its tower back, salt and pepper and their watchtower backs, bread basket,

## Order of work

1. Section 5 first, end to end, as the proof of the method (it has blocking and Robin's notes).
2. Sections 6 and 7 next: they share section 5's city and carry the film's two key images.
3. Then 1 to 4 and 8 to 10, in parallel, one builder each, on the shared engine and piece set.
4. Join, review as one film, fix, then show Robin. Seedance only after that, on his number.
