Beat sync

Pick a run and press Play (or the space bar). The element is drawn live over the picture, so every change shows straight away. The strip under the picture is the beat grid: one cell per 16th note, a thin line on each beat, a thick line on each bar. Gold cells are hits. Click a cell to add or remove a hit; hold the mouse and drag to paint several in, or out. Click the time ruler on top of the strip to jump there. Slow it down to check an edge. When it looks right, tell Claude "done" (or press Copy cells).
