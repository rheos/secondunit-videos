# Romancing the Atom: public research (2026-10-19)

## What the film is
- **Format:** a feature documentary. Director Mark Achbar, producer Betsy Carson, executive producers Achbar and Carson. Credited as "inspired by Robert Del Tredici's photos and interviews". Listed as "in production with Knowledge Network and TVO". https://www.ihpi.ca/
- **Synopsis (IHP):** the film covers the moral, environmental and political consequences of nuclear weapons, past and future. It weaves Del Tredici's archive together with "contemporary explorations of today's nuclear landscape", and it draws a parallel between early nuclear development and the rise of AI. https://www.ihpi.ca/
- **Core question (Achbar):** humanity's "addiction" to existentially threatening technologies: nuclear weapons, fossil fuels, "arguably AI". He calls the film "more diagnostic than prescriptive", though he wants experts on how to break the addiction. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pGKUjpJCrsE
- **Stage:** Carson's CV lists it as "2016-2025 ... (in development)". https://facetofacemedia.ca/site25/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Betsy-full-CV-2025.pdf IHP says it is in production. In April-May 2026 Achbar was filming at the UN NPT Review Conference (YouTube above; dates at https://peaceboat.org/english/news/report-2026-npt-review-conference). He also filmed an anti-AI and data-centre protest in Vancouver.
- **Funders:** Knowledge Network and TVO (IHP). Wikipedia adds the Canada Media Fund and Creative BC, without a citation. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Achbar Not confirmed in any CMF or Creative BC record.
- **Not found:** runtime, release date, editor, DOP, composer, animators.

## Del Tredici's photographs
- Achbar helped produce *At Work in the Fields of the Bomb* (1987). His plan is to animate Del Tredici's photos and sync them to the original interview tapes, which sit in a storage locker. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pGKUjpJCrsE
- The book has 100+ black-and-white photos, each with a short text on the same page. Subjects include Edward Teller, Operation Crossroads veteran John Smitherman, and the Pantex plant. https://peacemagazine.org/archive/v04n3p25.htm
- Del Tredici shot the bomb plants from rented Cessnas above 2,000 ft. The storage locker turned up real film of him doing it (YouTube above).
- **Key images:** his 2021 Bulletin photo essay has 18 images with typed captions, written with Gordon Edwards. They include the Hanford Tank Farm, a Ball of Plutonium, Teller, Maids of Muslyumovo and a Hiroshima bomb replica. https://thebulletin.org/2021/10/how-do-humans-make-sense-of-the-bomb-a-photo-essay/
- **Title source:** the title comes from Del Tredici's own essay "Romancing the Atom" (*Views*, 1989; *Northern Lights*, 1990). https://atomicphotographersguild.org/archives/
- **Other books:** *The People of Three Mile Island*, and *Atomic America* (2017). He founded the Atomic Photographers Guild (1987). https://atomicphotographersguild.org/members/robert-del-tredici/
- Del Tredici taught the history of animated film at Concordia. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Del_Tredici

## The AI angle
- **Achbar on AI:** he calls AI "fundamentally different" because it can make its own choices. He says he "dove into ChatGPT head first", and he makes songs with Suno. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pGKUjpJCrsE
- **Demo reel:** it includes a Del Tredici photo animated with HeyGen ($29). It also has an AI shot of Bob in the Cessna, which took about 25 tries.
- **Pushback:** one funder turned the project down over the planned generative AI use. A UN interviewee refused to take part because she links AI to uranium demand.
- **His rule:** AI use will be "pretty self-referential, pretty obvious". He told the April 2026 BC+AI Film Club he is "an amateur in AI and animation". https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CePBYHfagyk (about 1:22:00)
- **LA shoot:** no public record found of the Nicholas Meyer or Alexandra Bell interviews. Bell has been Bulletin president and CEO since February 2025. https://thebulletin.org/2025/02/alexandra-bell-to-join-bulletin-as-president-ceo/

## The global appeal
- **Sponsors:** World BEYOND War, with RootsAction, On Earth Peace, Ground Zero Center and others.
- **The ask:** all nine nuclear states adopt no first use, sign the TPNW, and eliminate nuclear weapons by August 6, 2045.
- **Endorsers:** Daniel Ellsberg is quoted as an endorser. Backers include WILPF, Code Pink, Veterans For Peace and Pressenza.
- **Signatures:** 15,925 against a goal of 25,600 on 2026-10-19.
- Petition: https://actionnetwork.org/petitions/global-appeal-to-nine-nuclear-governments?nowrapper=true

## What would land (INFERENCE)
- **Archive first.** Treat the photos as documents. Animate to reveal, not invent: parallax, rephotography, push-ins, and type that echoes the book's photo-and-caption pages. Map the bomb complex, since Bob "put them on the map". Chart the warhead count falling from about 70,000 to about 12,400.
- **Offer two tracks.** One uses generative AI, disclosed on screen. The other is a non-generative craft fallback. A funder and an interviewee have already balked, so a fallback lowers his risk. Follow the Archival Producers Alliance GenAI guidelines: disclosure, cue sheets, credits. https://opendoclab.mit.edu/presents/archival-producers-alliance-apa-best-practices-use-generative-ai-documentaries/
- **Lean into the irony.** He enjoys "hall of mirrors" self-reference. Graphics that show their own making suit him.
- **Rhyme the two technologies.** Visually rhyme the bomb plants with data centres, for example Cessna aerials of the plants against data-centre aerials.
- **Pitch as a collaborator.** He experiments hands-on. Pitch as the person who takes his HeyGen tests to broadcast quality, with each model and step recorded, as the NFB advises (pGKUjpJCrsE).
- **Being BC-based helps.** Knowledge commissions first-window docs only from BC producers. https://about.knowledge.ca/independent-producers/documentary-commissions-and-second-windows/

**Avoid:**
- fake "archival" that could pass for real
- cloned voices of real people without consent
- glossy mushroom-cloud kitsch
- tech-bro AI gloss
- confusing the film with *The Atom: A Love Affair* (2019) or R.R. Johnson's 2012 book of the same title
