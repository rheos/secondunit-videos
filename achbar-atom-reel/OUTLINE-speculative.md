# Romancing the Atom: a speculative outline (ours, not Mark's)

Written 2026-10-19 to steer what the demo reel covers next. Everything here is INFERENCE from the public
record in `RESEARCH-romancing-the-atom.md`, Del Tredici's books, and the shape of Mark's earlier films
(`references/mark-achbar/devices.md`). Nothing below is confirmed by the production. Facts cited for the
reel still need the same checking as before; "to verify" marks what we have not checked yet.

## What constrains the shape

- The title is Del Tredici's 1989 essay title. "Romancing" is the thesis: seduction first, then the
  hangover. Mark's own word for the hangover is "addiction," and he says the film is "diagnostic."
- The spine is Del Tredici's archive: 1980s photographs of the US bomb complex and the people in it, with
  his interview tapes from the same visits. Mark wants the photos animated and synced to the tapes.
- Mark's films run as two-column arguments with chapter cards (The Corporation: 23 clinical chapters,
  diagnosis to prognosis) and end on what a viewer can do (both films). Expect the same spine here:
  the atom's story told alongside AI's, chapter by chapter, with a hopeful last act.
- New shooting we know of: the UN NPT Review Conference (Apr-May 2026), a Vancouver anti-data-centre
  protest, and the LA interviews with Nicholas Meyer (The Day After) and Alexandra Bell (the Bulletin).

## The outline, as chapter cards

### Act I: the romance

1. **Prologue: the photographer.** Bob Del Tredici in the storage locker with the tapes; the 1980s
   Cessna aerials of the plants; "I wanted to put them on the map." The film announces its method:
   old pictures, old voices, new life.
2. **Attraction.** The language of love around the atom: "greatest thing in history," Atoms for Peace,
   "too cheap to meter," the 3-cent stamp, the atomic-age kitchen, the 1950s civil-defence films. Then
   the same language around AI, 2014-2025. (The reel's plates 1 and 2 already do this.)
3. **The Fields.** The bomb complex as a landscape: Hanford, Oak Ridge, Rocky Flats, Pantex, Fernald,
   Savannah River, the Nevada Test Site, Los Alamos, each a Del Tredici photograph with a worker's
   voice. The scale of the enterprise, built in secret, in ordinary towns.
4. **The first warning.** Trinity, the atmosphere-ignition question, Compton's three in a million,
   Fermi's bet, Szilard's petition. The makers knew. (Plates 3 and 7 of the reel.)

### Act II: the addiction

5. **The habit.** Stockpiles to 70,000; testing; the downwinders and the veterans (Del Tredici's John
   Smitherman, Operation Crossroads). Cost, bodies, land. The oscilloscope curve.
6. **The Day After.** Nicholas Meyer and the 1983 ABC film that 100 million people watched (to verify:
   air date 20 Nov 1983, audience figure, Reagan's diary line). Mass media as the only time the public
   felt it. A chance for Mark's old trick: the broadcast interrupting itself.
7. **Teller.** The man who argued for the bigger bomb, photographed by Del Tredici, as the archetype of
   the addict who always wants the next dose. His AI counterparts are the accelerationists.
8. **The relapse.** The Doomsday Clock at 85 seconds; the Bulletin's Bell on why AI is now in the
   statement; nine nuclear states modernising; the NPT conference going nowhere. (Plate 6.)
9. **The new dealer.** AI's own arms race: "Winning the Race," the compute race, data centres, and the
   loop back to the atom: Three Mile Island Unit 1 to be restarted to power Microsoft's data centres
   (to verify: Constellation, announced Sept 2024, "Crane Clean Energy Center"); Amazon, Google and Meta
   buying nuclear; the uranium demand that made a UN interviewee refuse Mark. The Vancouver protest.
10. **p(doom).** The makers' own odds, 1945 and 2024; Hinton's tiger cub; the "foom" argument on both
    sides. (Plate 7.)

### Act III: the diagnosis, and the treatment

11. **The pathology.** Mark's diagnostic frame: addiction criteria applied to a civilisation (the
    Corporation's checklist, redone). Tolerance, denial, relapse, harm to others. (Plate 8, red flags.)
12. **Who got clean.** Countries that gave up weapons (South Africa; Ukraine, Belarus, Kazakhstan after
    1991; to verify), the test-ban, the TPNW states, the people in the halls at the NPT conference.
    Fairy Creek-style activists for the data-centre fight.
13. **The appeal.** IHP's global appeal to the nine nuclear governments (no first use, sign the TPNW,
    eliminate by 6 Aug 2045), and whatever the AI equivalent is by release. Ends pointing at the door,
    like both earlier films.
14. **Coda: the atom, still romantic.** Del Tredici's own ambivalence: the pictures are beautiful. The
    film admits it, which is the honest version of the title.

## Where Mark will put graphics (inference)

Because his interview structure gets locked first and pictures come after (TC10), the graphics will land
on spoken lines. The slots most likely to exist, in rough order of how sure we are:

1. Bringing the photographs to life: parallax, push-ins, the sync to the tapes. The core ask. We cannot
   use his photos in a spec piece, so the reel shows the manner on public-domain stills instead.
2. The map of the bomb complex, and the same map with the data centres on it.
3. The two-column rhyme, atom beside AI, in every chapter (the reel's whole method).
4. The numbers: stockpile, warheads, the clock, the compute curve (Epoch AI via OWID), nuclear power
   deals for data centres, the p(doom) figures.
5. Chapter cards in a clinical register, and one recurring self-referential graphic that shows its own
   making (the drafting pen).
6. Archive used against itself: 1950s civil-defence film, 2020s keynote clips (he already enjoys this).

## What the reel does not cover yet, ranked for the next pass

1. **The map.** The complex as a blueprint map of the US with Del Tredici's sites pinned (his book's
   itinerary is public), then the same map with today's data-centre and nuclear-restart sites. Two
   columns on one sheet. Zero credits, every pin sourceable.
2. **The restart.** Three Mile Island as the hinge: Del Tredici's second book was The People of Three
   Mile Island; the plant is coming back to run AI. One archival print (TMI photos exist in the public
   domain from the NRC, to verify) and one dated announcement.
3. **The Day After.** A TV-broadcast plate: a 1983 living room, the CRT, the audience number, the
   diary line; the broadcast cut off by a commercial (MC09).
4. **The compute curve on the scope**, CH2 beside the warheads (Epoch AI via OWID, download the CSV).
5. **The photo-to-tape sync demo**, done on a public-domain portrait with a public-domain voice (an
   NBC Oppenheimer clip is not PD; find a government recording), to show the manner without his assets.
6. **A chapter-card system**: the clinical titles above as a title style, so he can see the film's
   skeleton in our type.
7. **The treatment**: a last plate that is hopeful, the appeal's three asks as a checklist that can be
   ticked, which is also the only plate that should wait for his say.

Skip: anything with his photographs, cloned voices, mushroom-cloud montage, a fake trailer.
