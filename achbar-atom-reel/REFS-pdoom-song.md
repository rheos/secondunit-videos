# The references in "I'm Upping My P(doom)"

The song Robin sent Mark (the "Claude-Pop" re-sung version by deckard, Suno, Sept 2026; lyrics by osmarks on an
opening verse and chorus by MusicPerson with the EleutherAI Discord, verses written around 17 Apr 2024, the rest
8-10 Nov 2024; original Udio render Nov 2024). Compiled 2026-10-19 from osmarks' own line-by-line annotation
(docs.osmarks.net/hypha/p(doom)_song_objectively_correct_interpretation) and the timed lyric file in
mexicat/pdoom-video. No lyric lines here; this is the index of what the lines point at, in order. 46 lines: four
verses, four choruses, an outro; p(doom) is the refrain and every chorus adds a reason to raise it.
