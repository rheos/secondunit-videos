# Machine-elf space: what reports say, against the living loop

Research for draft 6, 2026-10-25. Robin is drawing on his own memory of the DMT world, specifically the "machine
elves" kind of space. A research agent read the phenomenology literature and compared it with draft 5; this is a
summary of its report. Figures are the agent's reading of the sources, not re-checked line by line.

Sources: Lawrence et al. 2022, 3,778 Reddit reports (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9130218/);
Michael, Luke & Robinson 2021, 36 field interviews (https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.720717/full);
Davis et al. 2020, 2,561 survey respondents; McKenna's 1983 Esalen lecture "Tryptamine Hallucinogens and
Consciousness" (Erowid PDF); Qualia Research Institute on DMT geometry (https://qri.org/blog/hyperbolic-geometry-DMT).

## The cluster

No study offers a formal typology; grouping the tables gives a machine-elf / jester / carnival cluster of perhaps
10-15% of entity encounters (elves, faeries, jesters and clowns, children; carnival or playhouse settings; dancing,
singing, playful beings). Other clusters to keep out: the waiting room (an enclosed room with a futuristic
contraption), the medical / insectoid operating room (sterile rooms, scanning, implants, mantis beings), the
feminine / divine-love encounters, the cosmic void, and the pre-breakthrough "chrysanthemum" of wallpaper symmetry.

McKenna's account is the primary text for the cluster: an enclosed, domed space; a room that turns ("merry go
raum"); a childlike feeling; elves as constantly transforming topological forms who sing, and whose sound becomes
visible as rotating objects of light; they urge the visitor to copy them.

## Draft 5 against the reports

| Element | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Sigils of light that fold into solids | Strong match: McKenna's visible language, but from the voice, not the hands |
| Floating polyhedra | Very common (geometric objects in 44% of Michael's interviews) |
| Tesseracts | A recurring trope (a "hyperdimensional cube" across five of Michael's participants) |
| Small childlike beings at playful work | Fits the cluster (Munchkins, a three-foot Gumby in a nursery); reported elves shape-shift |
| Purposeful machinery | Reported, but in the elf cluster the work is play and display |
| Releasing objects into the world | Partly against: beings mostly present objects to the visitor and start the encounter |
| Matte pastel clay | Against on light and surface: vivid, self-lit, jewel-like colour (about a quarter of reports), synthetic, retro-game and holographic textures (42% in Michael) |
| Calm motion | Too calm: bounding, dancing, constantly transforming |
| Open sky, clouds, hills | Weakest fit: the elf space is enclosed and domed |
| Screen-and-lever console | Leans toward the waiting-room cluster |

## Decisions for draft 6 (Robin, 2026-10-25)

- The casters face the viewer and offer the solid before it drifts off; other workers turn and beckon.
- The sigil is sung: glyphs stream from the mouth; the hands conduct.
- The world closes into a slowly turning honeycomb dome; clouds and open sky go; the window sun is a lantern at the top.
- The elves bounce and shape-shift; a small chorus, some in harlequin diamonds and bell caps, cheers each offering.
- Mark Von Ulrich's world comes in through what the beings make and show (glyphs, sigils, solids, the funnel's
  inside, the console display), in his saturated early-Mac pixel language against the quiet clay. The research's
  "synthetic, garish, retro-game" colour is close to his surface. The file names "machine elves" and "this goes into
  this" on two of his pieces in `references/mark-von-ulrich/artwork/` are Robin's, not Mark's titles.

## Robin's diva and the placard road (follow-up, for a separate piece)

Robin's memory: a central diva figure (a Kokopelli or Fido Dido silhouette) moves her arms and a shape appears
between them; every time her arms cross, a new shape appears; dozens of smaller gnomes extend away up a road, all
holding placards with the same shape.

- A feminine guide who shows things is the most common entity type: feminine entities 24.2% and "guiding,
  touring or showing" 26.6% (Lawrence 2022); "presenter" in 10 of 36 interviews (Michael 2021).
- Shapes in response to gesture: communication by gesture 40% (Davis 2020); one account of shapes and flowers
  emerging from an entity's solar plexus (Michael); McKenna's elves make objects of light from song.
- Crowds are common (more than one entity in about three quarters of Michael's reports; hundreds or thousands in
  some accounts). No study reports beings echoing a central figure in unison.
- Closest parallel to the placards: Strassman's volunteer Karl met mischievous elves beside an interstate highway
  who showed him swirling geometric scenes on placards (reached through a secondary summary,
  https://psychedelicpress.substack.com/p/faerie-dmt, not the book text).
- The unison relay down a receding road appears to be Robin's own detail; nothing in the literature contradicts it.
