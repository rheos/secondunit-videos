## ref-01-bug-doll-stage.png (2026-10-25, Robin: "this will be a reference for this project")

*The image file is not in the folder yet: Robin sent it inline in chat and no file landed on disk. Drop it in as
`references/ref-01-bug-doll-stage.png`. The description below is from the chat copy.*

A pop-surrealist painting (Ryden / lowbrow manner). A doll-like seated figure with a round beetle-dome head: huge
dark saucer eyes, a small painted mouth under the dome, two striped antennae curling at the tips and three stalks
with balls on top; an ornate patterned bodice, a fringed and tasselled skirt, scale-blue tail-fins at the hem. Two
pink plumed insect-birds on thin legs with antennae and layered eyes, one on a small stand; a moth-fairy with a
face in flight; small bees. A shallow stage: ochre floor, a teal-to-green gradient backdrop in a round arch, a dark
oxblood ornamental frame curling in at the edges.

What it offers:
- **Figures**: dome heads with saucer eyes instead of our dot-eyed clay heads; antennae and stalks with ball tips;
  ornament on the costume (pattern, fringe, tassels, fins); creatures that are part insect, part bird, part doll.
- **Palette and value**: deep teal and green behind, ochre floor, dusty pink creatures, oxblood frame, cream skin;
  the figures are saturated and lit against a dark ground, with real darks. The audit of draft 40 said our world
  reads pale and single-valued; this is the direction that answers it.
- **Staging**: a proscenium (round arch in an ornate frame) and a floor plane; the beings posed and watching us.
- **Material**: painted, smooth, glossy highlights on the dome and eyes; not clay, so for us the lesson is the
  shapes and the colour, rendered crisp in our kit.

Not to take: the exact figure, the Ryden face, the composition.
