# Bob Wiseman, "In Her Dream"

Title track of his 1989 debut, *In Her Dream: Bob Wiseman Sings Wrench Tuttle* (reissued 2024 by
Indie Pool). Album version on YouTube: sOIiEc7C6V8 (Bob Wiseman - Topic), 5:00.

Direction from Robin (2026-10-04): painted, in the manner of Van Gogh's swirling night skies
(directional strokes that follow the forms, halos around lights), with a wider palette than his
blue/yellow. The 1989 cover (a woman in pink holding a small child's hand, flat naive shapes) is a
reference, not a brief: the video should look current.

Audio, lyrics, the cover scan and renders stay out of git (see .gitignore). The pinned master is
`source/in-her-dream.wav` with its sha256 beside it; private notes live in `source/brief.md`.
