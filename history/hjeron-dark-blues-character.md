## The character

Robin, 2026-09-28, sharing 14 reference images: "shift in direction. for the dark blues project

**The character:** faceless because the face sits in the SHADOW OF THE HAT BRIM. Black wide-brim leather hat with a braided band. **Robin 2026-09-28: his hair is NOT down in his face and NOT dreadlocks ("he's not jack sparrow.. or slash")**; the probe's thick dreadlock curtain over the face was wrong. Don't prompt a fringe/hair veil over the face; hide the face with brim shadow, angle and backlight. Robin: "it's more about the dark lighting than the face being obscured by hair. it's more in shadow." So: low-key lighting, deep shadow, silhouette and backlight do the hiding. long black fringed coat, fingerless
black leather gloves, black nail polish, a silver star-compass medallion and green beadwork on the
chest, black Les Paul-style guitar (Epiphone strap). **World:** Sonoran desert (palo verde,
creosote, gravel wash, distant purple ranges), a ram skull on a stone fire ring with charred logs,
red dirt, a rattlesnake, cracked earth, a wolfdog companion. **Light:** full moon at night, heavy
storm clouds, dusk and sunset silhouettes, low heroic angles. Two refs (a moonlit shot and a
serpent rising from burning cracked earth) carry an AI-generator sparkle mark, so they're mood
refs, not photos of him.

**Why:** cut 3 of "dream of fire" put his bare face, face paint and a fur mantle on screen with
face lipsync. That's the MythMaker-era look, not this act. A faceless character also means face
lipsync (the H3 method) doesn't fit as-is.

hands, guitar, hat and fringe. Use his real ViralVision footage
