Everything is code-drawn: a height field contoured with marching squares at load (50 m interval, index every 250 m), relief shading from the north-west, hachures down steep fall lines, a cliff with a toothed rock line, blue water lines along the shore, a river with flow dashes, deciduous tree symbols, a dashed footpath, a kilometre grid, a graduated neatline, grid numbers and a scale bar. Numbers only; no words on screen.
