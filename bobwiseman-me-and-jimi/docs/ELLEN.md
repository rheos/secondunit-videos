# Ellen McIlwaine: checked facts (2026-10-04)

What the video can lean on. Each line names its source; anything not here is not checked.

- Born 1 October 1945, Nashville. Adopted by missionary parents (William and Aurine); taken to Kobe, Japan at two, lived there about 15 years. [Wikipedia; CelebrityAccess obit]
- Kobe: attended Canadian Academy, graduated 1963. Piano from five; played for her father's Presbyterian church; snare drum in the school marching band; school choir. [Wikipedia; Globe and Mail; CelebrityAccess]
- Radio: heard Japanese folk and classical music, and Ray Charles, Fats Domino and Professor Longhair, on Japanese radio; the Grand Ole Opry and European classical on American Armed Forces Radio. [her official bio, goddessofslide.com] (Globe and Mail credits the R&B to Armed Forces radio. Sources disagree; the radio is safe, the station is not.)
- Back to the US at 17 (1963): King College, Bristol TN, then art classes in Atlanta, where she found the guitar and played coffeehouses. [CelebrityAccess; Wikipedia]
- 1966, Greenwich Village: opened every night at the Cafe Au Go Go, played with Jimi Hendrix, opened for Muddy Waters, Sonny Terry and Brownie McGhee, Big Joe Williams. [Wikipedia] Six nights on stage with Hendrix. [Goddess of Slide documentary] She credited Hendrix with teaching her that what she had to express was unique. [Globe and Mail] Wrote "Underground River" about him. [Wikipedia]
- Fear Itself: psychedelic blues band from Atlanta, album recorded in Woodstock 1968; solo again 1970. [official bio; Wikipedia]
- Solo: Honky Tonk Angel (1972), We the People (1973), both Polydor. Everybody Needs It with Jack Bruce (1982). [Wikipedia]
- Learned open tunings from Johnny Winter. Known for acoustic slide and a multi-octave voice. [Globe and Mail; Wikipedia]
- To Canada in the 80s (Wikipedia: 1987, Toronto then Alberta; Globe: after getting sober in 1982, Montreal, Toronto, then Calgary). In Calgary she taught guitar and voice, volunteered at Alberta Children's Hospital, and DROVE A SCHOOL BUS. [Globe and Mail]
- Died 23 June 2021 in Calgary, esophageal cancer diagnosed six weeks earlier. [Wikipedia; Globe and Mail]
- Documentary: Goddess of Slide: The Forgotten Story of Ellen McIlwaine (Alfonso Maiorana, 2024, CBC Gem). [Wikipedia]
- NOT confirmed: any Bonnie Raitt connection. Don't use it.
