# Blues sources for the theatre-seat cutouts

All from Wikimedia Commons, fetched 2026-10-05. Each file is either the original or a ~1600 px Commons thumbnail, whichever is smaller. "Size" is the original's size on Commons.

CC BY and CC BY-SA images need the author credited in the video credits or description. CC BY-SA also asks that a derivative (the woodcut piece) go out under the same licence. Public domain needs nothing, but crediting the source is still good practice.

## Muddy Waters

**muddy-1.jpg**
- Page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Muddy_Waters_november_1976.jpg
- Author: Lionel Decoster. Date: 7 Nov 1976. Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0. Size 1040x1500.
- Frame: waist-up on stage, playing his Telecaster, patterned shirt and vest, looking off left. Black background, easy cut. Crop out the mic stand at left. This is the Wikipedia infobox photo.

**muddy-2.jpg**
- Page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Muddy_Waters_1976.jpg
- Author: Lionel Decoster. Date: 7 Nov 1976. Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0. Size 1030x1500.
- Frame: same concert, a tighter shot. Head and chest, a squinting, gruff look while he plays the Telecaster. Dark background with stage-light streaks at top right. Mic at the left edge, crop it out.

## Lightnin' Hopkins

**hopkins-1.jpg**
- Page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lightnin_Hopkins_1960s_LOC.jpg
- Author: American Folklife Center, Library of Congress. Date: 1963. Licence: public domain.
- Size 792x952, a halftone print scan.
- Frame: his classic look. Pork-pie hat, round dark glasses, big grin, suit and tie, acoustic guitar neck going off the right edge. Waist-up on a plain light-grey background, a clean cut. The halftone dots show when you zoom in, which suits a woodcut anyway.

**hopkins-2.jpg**
- Page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lightnin%27_Hopkins.jpg
- Author: 33stradale. Date on file: 2011-04-20 (upload date; the photo itself is 1960s). Licence: CC BY-SA 3.0. Size 2000x2996.
- Frame: seated, dark wraparound shades, bow tie and pocket square, reading a magazine on his lap. No hat, no guitar. The background is a dim room with hard shadows, so the cut is harder than hopkins-1. Use it as a backup or for a second pose.

## Little Richard

**richard-1.jpg**
- Page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:LittleRichard1960s.jpg
- Author: Okeh Records (publicity photo, printed in a 1969 newspaper). Licence: public domain (no copyright notice). Size 700x830.
- Frame: head and shoulders, mouth wide open mid-shout, the high pompadour. Plain light background. It's small and a coarse newspaper halftone, but it is the most "Little Richard" image on Commons. Good as a woodcut silhouette; fine face detail is soft.

**richard-2.jpg**
- Page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Little_Richard_1984_(cropped).jpg
- Author: Ian Dryden, Los Angeles Times (UCLA Library). Date: 11 Dec 1984. Licence: CC BY 4.0. Size 1316x1678.
- Frame: head and shoulders, serious look into the camera, track jacket. Plain grey background, sharp, clean cut. The catch: his hair is a short 1980s curl, not the pompadour, so he is less recognisable. Better detail, weaker icon.

## Chuck Berry

## Janis Joplin

**joplin-1.jpg**
- Page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Janis_Joplin_seated_1970.JPG
- Author: Albert B. Grossman Management (publicity photo). Date: about 1969-70. Licence: public domain (no notice). Size 1103x1400.
- Frame: seated on a stool, big fur hat over long hair, bead strands, loose blouse, laughing, one hand out. Plain grey studio background, an easy cut. Already a seated pose, which helps for a theatre seat. No round glasses in any free photo; see the report.

**joplin-2.jpg**
- Page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Janis_Joplin_1970.JPG
- Author: Grossman Glotzer Management Corporation. Date: 26 Jun 1970. Licence: public domain (no notice). Size 629x758, small.
- Frame: waist-up, long wild hair, bell sleeves, a tie-dye top, beads, hand on hip, smiling. Plain light background, a very clean cut. Low resolution, so the face is about 120 px wide.

## Sister Rosetta Tharpe

**tharpe-1.jpg**
- Page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sister_Rosetta_Tharpe_(1938_publicity_photo_with_guitar).jpg
- Author: James J. Kriegsmann. Date: 1938. Licence: public domain. Size 855x1049.
- Frame: full body, standing, resonator guitar held high, long patterned gown, looking up and smiling. Light background with her shadow on the wall behind. Crop to waist-up with the guitar. The face is small at this crop (about 90 px), but the guitar pose is the icon.

**tharpe-2.jpg**
- Page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sister_Rosetta_Tharpe_(1938_publicity_photo_-_headshot).jpg
- Author: James J. Kriegsmann. Date: 1938. Licence: public domain. Size 919x1024.
- Frame: a close headshot, head tilted, smiling, looking up and to the side, curled hair. Soft grey background. Sharp face and an easy cut, but no guitar. Pair it with tharpe-1's guitar if you need both.

## 80s icons and Otis Spann (added 2026-10-05; Robin: any good source is fine for carved pieces, sources noted)

# 1980s pop icon sources (Me and Jimi, "I heard about her in the 80s")

Reference photos for the woodcut cut-out dancers under the mirror ball. All ten came from Wikimedia Commons and all are free licences (public domain, CC BY or CC BY-SA). Partway through, the brief dropped the licence rule (2026-10-05). That didn't change any pick: none of the non-free sources found beat these for an 80s look, clean background and resolution.

Saved as JPEG. The PNG originals (prince-2) were flattened onto white.

## Cyndi Lauper

**lauper-1.jpg**
- Page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cyndi_Lauper_-_The_Roanoke_Times_(1984).jpg
- Author: Wayne Deel for The Roanoke Times. Date: 3 Dec 1984 (concert). Licence: public domain. Size 1528x2881.
- Frame: full body, mid-dance on stage, knees bent, one hand up. Big teased blonde hair with a bow, round dark glasses, long plaid coat, fishnets, ankle boots. Dark stage background. It's a newspaper halftone, so the dot screen shows, but the woodcut will swallow that. Best dancing pose of the set.

**lauper-2.jpg**
- Page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:We_Are_The_World_Cyndi-Glenn_Francis.jpg
- Author: Toglenn (Glenn Francis). Date: 28 Jan 1985, We Are the World session. Licence: CC BY-SA 3.0. Size 1148x1187, colour.
- Frame: chest-up, head tilted, spiky dyed yellow-orange hair, chandelier earrings, stacks of coloured bead necklaces, white fluffy top. A man (the photographer) stands beside her, so crop to the left third. Her figure there is only about 550 px wide. This is the definitive 1985 look, but it's static, so take the head and costume from here and the body from lauper-1.

## Bruce Springsteen

**springsteen-1.jpg**
- Page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bruce_Springsteen_Born_in_the_U.S.A._Tour_photo_by_Rocky_Widner.jpg
- Author: Rocky Widner. Date: 1985, Born in the U.S.A. Tour. Licence: public domain. Size 897x1142.
- Frame: thigh-up, right fist punched in the air, singing into the mic, Telecaster slung low, sleeveless shirt, short hair. Dark stage background, with a band member's head at bottom right to paint out. The iconic BUSA fist pump.

**springsteen-2.jpg**
- Page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bundesarchiv_Bild_183-1988-0719-38,_Bruce_Springsteen,_Konzert_in_der_DDR.jpg
- Author: Thomas Uhlemann (Bundesarchiv). Date: 19 Jul 1988, East Berlin concert. Licence: CC BY-SA 3.0 DE. Size 506x797, greyscale, small.
- Frame: waist-up, playing the Telecaster, black waistcoat over a white tee, cross necklace, studded strap, looking up. A busy blurred scaffold background, but it's soft, so the cut is fine. Low resolution, with the face about 110 px wide. It's 1988 rather than 84-85, though the look is barely different.
- Considered: "Bruce Springsteen at Giants Stadium.jpg" (1985, CC BY-SA 4.0), which is the right era but a tiny figure in a wide stage shot.

## Boy George

**boygeorge-1.jpg**
- Page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Culture_Club_(1984_HBO_publicity_photo).jpg
- Author: HBO, distributed by Epic Records. Date: 1983/84. Licence: public domain. Size 1155x1299.
- Frame: a group shot. George sits centre on a stool, full body, in a wide-brim hat, ribboned braids, heavy eye make-up and a long pale coat. The other three stand around him in baseball kit with bats, and one bat crosses his chest and needs painting out. Flag backdrop. He is about 900 px tall. Seated, so the dance pose needs inventing.
- Alternative: "Culture Club 1983.jpg" (Mark Lebon/Epic, public domain, 2246x1711). George is crouched at the far left in a hat and braids, partly cut off, against a light background.

## Otis Spann

Added 2026-10-05 after the licence rule was dropped (the photos get carved into woodcut and heavily altered). Nothing free exists, so this is a non-free file.

**spann-1.jpg**
- Page: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Otis_Spann.jpg (file: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/9/9f/Otis_Spann.jpg)
- Author: unknown, a publicity photo (Wikipedia credits allmusic.com). Date: 1960s. Licence: fair use on en.wikipedia, NOT free. Size 270x369, very small.
- Frame: head and shoulders, white shirt, dark tie, hand at chin with a cigarette, looking at camera. Light, plain background, easy cut. The face is about 110 px wide, so it only works small or heavily stylised.
- Alternatives seen, both tiny and watermarked: at the piano, eyes shut, singing (Chuck Stewart, https://www.wirz.de/music/spann/grafik/spann21.jpg, 308x370); at the piano with James Cotton, 1965 (Ray Flerlage, https://www.wirz.de/music/spann/grafik/spann31.jpg, 370x354). Getty has ~49 larger shots (https://www.gettyimages.com/photos/otis-spann), all watermarked.
